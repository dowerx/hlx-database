Overview

In this short HLX Files entry, Tyler argues that Valve employees have repeatedly hinted at Half-Life 3’s existence and that the accumulated evidence already establishes the project. He cites a reported unnamed employee comment about being “in the room,” a chat-thread exchange during production of his Half-Life 3 documentary, a Ben Burbank developer talk touching on gravity systems and NPC communication, and an old Ars Technica forum post. He also revisits the Deadlock HEV suit datamine and Mike Morasky’s denial of involvement with Deadlock’s music. The video contains no new build numbers, filenames, maps, or visual material; its release-timing discussion is explicitly framed as speculation.
