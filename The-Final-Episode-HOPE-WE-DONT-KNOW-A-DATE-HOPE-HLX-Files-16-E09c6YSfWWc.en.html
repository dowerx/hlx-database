Overview

Published on the day Valve announced the Steam Frame, Steam Machine, and Steam Controller, this episode covers that hardware announcement, re-examines the December 2024 hardware predictions, debunks a 4chan HLX concept-art leak and the circulating November 18 announcement date, and walks through Counter-Strike 2 string leaks. Tyler states repeatedly that nothing short of a Valve announcement should be treated as fact, and confirms that his expectation of a 2025 HLX announcement is unchanged while conceding Valve could slip into January or February. He also reports that Jason Schreier publicly clarified that his own 2025 Half-Life prediction was not based on inside information. Tyler closes by suggesting this may be the final HLX Files episode and that a follow-up will cover datamined material under the codename “TF.”

Mentioned events

Valve announces Steam Frame, Steam Machine, and Steam Controller for 2026

  • Description: Valve announced three hardware products together: the Steam Frame (VR headset), the Steam Machine (console), and the Steam Controller (previously leaked as the “Ibex” controller). Tyler says all three are “set to be released at some point in 2026,” and that in the official press release “that Casey put out within the Valve press list, Valve is aiming for an early 26 release.” He describes the industry reaction as treating the announcement as one of the biggest leaps forward in customer freedom in gaming.
  • Confidence: Fact — the announcement and the stated early-2026 target are attributed to Valve’s own press release; Tyler explicitly frames only the surrounding interpretation as speculation.
  • Category: Hardware
  • Source: Official Valve press release circulated on the Valve press list, as described by Tyler.
  • Earlier mentions: References the December 20, 2024 video “HLX Files Guide Den,” which predicted Valve was working on both a headset and a console; the prediction is presented as now confirmed.
  • Verification: verified — the announcement and early-2026 window can be checked against Valve’s press release and press coverage.

He says he will continue to err on the side of calling it more expensive so a lower price is a pleasant surprise.

4chan HLX concept art leak judged fake

  • Description: An anonymous 4chan poster claimed to have key art and concept art developed around 2022 for the “heavily rumored HLX project,” depicting real-world Eastern European environments being overtaken by the Borealis, the Aperture Science teleportation-testing ship taken over by the Combine after Half-Life 2. Tyler calls it fake: well-made fan art photobashed from real-world stock elements plus screenshots from Portal 1 and Half-Life Alyx. His key tell is the use of Portal 1 art, since Valve’s canonical Aperture look post-Portal 2 is Portal 2’s.

Jason Schreier clarifies his 2025 Half-Life prediction was not inside information

  • Description: Tyler reads from a Half-Life subreddit post by Jason Schreier, who wrote: “As some of you may know, I report on the video games industry and often break news based on inside information. As some of you may have seen, thanks to Tyler McVicker and other hype merchants, I predicted at the beginning of this year that a new Half-Life would be announced.” Schreier continued that his predictions are explicitly not based on inside information, as stated each year on his Triple Click podcast, and that “I have no idea whether or not Half-Life is being announced this year.” Asked why he clarified, Schreier said he takes his track record of accuracy seriously and did not want people saying he got something wrong when he never reported it. Asked what prompted the prediction, he cited the ending of Half-Life Alyx making clear Valve wants to keep going and the roughly six years since that game, about as long as big games take.
  • Confidence: Fact — quoted directly from Schreier’s public subreddit posts read aloud in the video.
  • Category: Release date
  • Source: Jason Schreier’s public posts in the Half-Life subreddit, quoted in the video.
  • Earlier mentions: References Schreier’s beginning-of-2025 prediction that a new Half-Life would be announced; this video reports that prediction was never based on reporting.
  • Verification: verified — the subreddit posts can be located and read directly.
