Overview

The closing episode of Tyler’s “Miro board” series collects Valve’s own public statements about a future Half-Life rather than engine datamining, arguing that they corroborate the technical picture assembled in the earlier episodes. It strings together interviews from around the March 2020 launch of Half-Life: Alyx (USgamer, TheGamer, VGC, Kotaku, Polygon, Eurogamer, Fandom), later remarks from Gabe Newell, Greg Coomer, Robin Walker and Erik Wolpaw through 2022, and 2024 comments from composer Mike Morasky and the Noclip Half-Life 2 documentary. Almost all of the material is quoted from named, published interviews; Tyler’s own interpretation is confined to framing (for example, that the résumé lines and Morasky’s “can’t talk about it” refer to HLX). The video adds little in the way of new artifacts and functions mainly as a compilation and recap of previously public statements.
