Overview

This video is primarily a Steam hardware status report covering Steam Machine and Steam Frame, not a Half-Life 3 update. Tyler walks through early-June 2026 evidence — Steam client and SteamVR beta datamines, Canadian radio certification, native Android builds for VR titles, and US customs imports — as signs that pre-orders and pricing are close. He relays an anonymous June 12 insider claim that prices and reservations will be announced between June 22 and 30, with a June 23 review embargo, and expects sales in early July. He is confident the hardware launches in June but explicitly labels his price and supply reasoning as assumption, and states that HLX information will not be tied to the hardware announcement.
