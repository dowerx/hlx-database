Overview

This installment is primarily an argument rather than a report: Tyler uses the episode to publicly reject the community “ARG 197” theory that Mike Shapiro’s New Year greeting and jazz discography encode a Half-Life 3 announcement. He frames the episode with the history of Valve’s Portal 2 and Potato Sack ARGs to argue that Valve no longer runs alternate reality games and instead announces projects with a single social media post. The only forward-looking claims are restated positions — that HLX is in a very late stage, already being tested by third-party players, and might be announced within the calendar year — offered without new evidence. He is emphatic and dismissive about the ARG theory and correspondingly confident, but unevidenced, about the development timeline.

He lists these as “just the things related to 197” and rejects the conclusion.
