Overview

Episode 4 of Tyler McVicker’s “Miro board” series catalogs enemy, NPC, and location references found in HLX data, framed as light spoilers. It walks through creature names recovered from strings and hint nodes (Houndeye, Xen Gorilla, Xen Jellyfish, Xen Swooper, Feral Manhack, Kush Child, Howler, Flitter, Harvest Man Baby) and Combine units (Charger, snipers, penta tank, workers, Storks), then discusses Xen, the Combine off-world, and a foundry as likely visitable places. The video also describes new hint-node-driven gameplay behaviors: designer-placed shield deployments, dynamic sniper positions, NPC-run holdouts, and NPCs dynamically picking up and dropping physics props. Tyler’s confidence is mixed: the existence of strings and hint nodes is presented as datamined evidence, while interpretations of what the creatures are and which locations the player visits are explicitly speculative.
