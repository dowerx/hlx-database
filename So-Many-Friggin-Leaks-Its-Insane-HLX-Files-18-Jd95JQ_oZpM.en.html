Overview

Tyler walks through two 2026 Source 2 updates — a Deadlock update from February 5 and a Dota 2 update from March 24 — that together produced an unusually large volume of HLX-related engine and tooling strings. The Deadlock build contains a particle-based volumetric smoke system that names HLX explicitly, while the Dota 2 build is described as the largest dump of HLX-adjacent Source 2 back-end data he has seen, including new tool asset icons, a Fountain screenplay-format tool, GPU particle-lighting features reserved for HLX, and expanded vehicle, prop-physics, and companion-system entities. Threaded through the datamine is Tyler’s argument that heavy choreography/cutscene tooling does not necessarily mean the game is early, supported by the Half-Life 2 leak timeline. The back half covers community mods (John’s Campaign, Gore Agulation, the Raising the Bar family) and a short note that Eric Wolpaw gave interviews he had not yet reviewed. Confidence is high on the datamined strings and icons themselves, lower on what they imply about the game’s content or schedule.

  • Description: Tyler notes that many people read the cutscene/choreography tooling as evidence the game is much further behind than previously thought, then argues the opposite: Half-Life 2 leaked about a year before release with no choreography work completed — only stopgap cutscenes and voice acting — and animated first-person choreography was not shown until E3 2004.
