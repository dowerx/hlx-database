Appendix: per-video event statistics

The database contains 610 events across 31 videos (2024-08-04 to 2026-10-22). Counts describe how finely each video was summarized, not how reliable it is. The per-event confidence and verification fields are the reliability measures.

Classification used here: Fact, Mixed, Speculation and Unclear are the first word of each event’s confidence field; a handful of hybrid labels are counted under their first term. Verification splits verified (directly checkable), partly (verified for some sub-claims, follow-up for others), follow-up (plausible, artifact not identified) and unverified (rests on unnamed sources).

5. Extremes and outliers

  • Densest summary: One-off (13 events in 2:56, 44.3 per 10 min).
  • Sparsest summary: One-off (58 events in 75:07, 7.7 per 10 min).
  • Most Fact-labeled: Every Last Detail Ep7 (25⁄28, 89%).
  • Most Speculation-labeled: HLX Files #7 (8⁄15, 53%).
  • Most unresolved: Every Last Detail Ep4 (23⁄23 events marked needs-follow-up).

Outliers usually reflect the video’s format, not its evidentiary value. The retrospective and interview episodes pack many discrete quotations; hardware one-offs pack prices and dates; a short reaction video can be mostly commentary.

6. Known gaps in coverage

The 31-video set is complete for the series as published. HLX Files #3 and #13 and Every Last Detail Ep3 have no counterpart on the channel — the series run #0–2, #4–12, #14–20 and Ep1–2, Ep4–7 — so the numbering jumps are not missing installments. Remaining gaps are only:

  • Any video published after the newest entry here (2026-10-22, HLX Files #20).
