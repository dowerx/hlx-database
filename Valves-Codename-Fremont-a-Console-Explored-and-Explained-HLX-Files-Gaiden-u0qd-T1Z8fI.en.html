Overview

This Gaiden episode recaps the earlier “Galileo” Steam Deck hardware datamine and presents newly found references to a device codenamed Fremont. Tyler describes a public mirror of Valve’s SteamOS and Steam Deck GitLab repositories, called “evav” in the transcript, where commits reference an AMD “Lilac” APU, a prototype board codenamed F7F, a full-sized HDMI-A port, and an HDMI CEC endpoint. He treats the datamined identifiers as factual evidence but frames the conclusion that Fremont is a living-room Steam console as an assumption. The video contains no Half-Life 3-specific information; Half-Life: Alyx appears only in the recap of a Valve employee’s photographs.
