Overview

Tyler reacts to a new in-character G-Man post from voice actor Mike Shapiro, published on New Year’s Eve 2024 after roughly four years of silence on the account, and uses it as a hook for an HLX status update. He declines to state what the post means and instead outlines the evidence he is watching: a colleague’s recent report that Valve has entered external play testing, Valve’s history of late-stage rewrites, and the risk that projects do not survive the holiday break. He predicts an announcement by the end of 2025 if play testing goes smoothly, slipping to 2026 if core gameplay, story, or art direction require rework. The video is largely a recap of his established positions, with the Shapiro post and the 2025⁄2026 framing as the new material; nearly all game-specific claims are inference rather than documented evidence.
