Overview

In this introductory HLX Files video, Tyler McVicker argues that the project codenamed HLX is a full non-VR Half-Life game — possibly Half-Life 3 — rather than an engine feature or a save-file format. He presents a voice actor’s résumé leak revealing the casting codename “Project White Sands,” a second former-employee résumé describing 2020–2023 work on an unannounced title, and datamined strings from Dota 2 and Counter-Strike 2 that reference an HEV suit, “offworld,” feral manhacks, and Xen creatures. He also corrects earlier attributions of Source 2 features to Deadlock. Tyler says most of the material comes from datamines but explicitly labels the Half-Life 3 identification and the single-player/co-op question as speculation or unknown, and he notes that a larger, more comprehensive video is still in production.

He stresses that someone at the agency or the actor’s representation made a mistake, not necessarily the actor, and asks viewers not to identify or harass the individual.
  • Confidence: Fact — the claim rests on a named primary artifact, a voice actor’s résumé leak, which Tyler presents as real and says came from “a screw up by one of the voice actors on the project.”
