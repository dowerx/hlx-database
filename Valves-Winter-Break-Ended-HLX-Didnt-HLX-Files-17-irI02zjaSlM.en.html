Overview

A post-winter-break status video rather than a reveal: Valve returned from the holidays and shipped sizeable updates to Counter-Strike 2 and Deadlock, plus a mid-December Dota 2 patch, and Tyler walks through the Source 2 strings found in them. No new HLX milestone, date, or asset is reported; the datamined material is almost entirely refactoring of existing NPC, animation, audio, and physics systems into newer VData and AnimGraph 2 formats. Tyler reads this as a long polish pass and explicitly concludes that the game “is going to be the most polished thing that Valve has ever created,” a claim he frames as interpretation rather than evidence. The remainder covers the community’s post-2025 hype hangover and hoaxes, the Half-Life: Alyx soundtrack vinyl box set, and four Half-Life mod releases. Confidence is high on the named datamine strings and store listings, and openly speculative on HLX timing, CS2 team status, and what the polish implies.
