Overview

Tyler recaps the repeatedly delayed launch of Valve’s Steam Frame headset, arguing that the device has slipped from a summer 2025 announcement to an expected September 14, 2026 on-sale date with no confirmed price. He reports that VR developers were given a late-July deadline to prepare software, that review units arrived August 24 under a postponed embargo, and that the standalone port of Half-Life: Alyx will be bundled free with the hardware but is unrelated to HLX. The bulk of the video is analysis and price prediction rather than new leaked material: Tyler expects at least \(999, cites Qualcomm chip and memory/storage cost increases, and compares the situation to his \)1,119 Steam Machine purchase. His claims range from attributed reporting and a November 2025 Valve press briefing to explicit personal speculation about pricing and supply.
