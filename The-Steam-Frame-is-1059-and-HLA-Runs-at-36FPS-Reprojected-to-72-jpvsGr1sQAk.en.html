Overview

A short news segment covering two developments: Valve’s official Steam Frame pricing and reservation process, and a Digital Foundry report on the standalone Half-Life: Alyx port. Tyler lists the full regional price sheet and accessory pricing as published by Valve, then describes the reservation queue and its eligibility rules. He also relays that Half-Life: Alyx runs at a native 36 FPS with reprojection to 72 on the Steam Frame, per Digital Foundry’s interview with Robin Walker and Autumn Ashton, with no release date given. Pricing and reservation details are presented as official; the port’s timing and Tyler’s own hardware expectations are inference.
