Overview

This video opens Tyler’s “HLX Files” series, a piecemeal replacement for a single large 2024 Valve code datamining project. Its main new item is that Valve opened a fresh testing branch for Half-Life 2 shortly before the game’s 20th anniversary, which Tyler frames as a likely anniversary celebration but not necessarily a franchise tease. Tyler also relays secondhand claims about Valve having two non-Counter-Strike, non-Deadlock games in development, restates that the Deckard VR headset is still in production while HLX is not a VR game, and reports that Valve removed or renamed several HLX-related strings after his previous video. He is explicit that much of the framing is inference or rumor, and says he does not expect an HLX or Half-Life 3 announcement in calendar year 2024.

Half-Life 2 20th-anniversary testing branch

  • Description: Tyler reports that “only a couple of days ago” Valve opened “a brand new testing Branch for halflife 2,” ahead of the game’s 20th anniversary. He notes he deliberately waited until after Thursday to judge whether it was worth reporting, because “nine times out of 10 they’re going to launch something on a Thursday” and Thursday is Steam’s lowest-traffic day. Thursday passed with no launch, so the purpose of the branch remains unknown.
  • Confidence: Fact — the branch is presented as an observed Steam depot/branch change rather than an announcement; the anniversary interpretation is Tyler’s inference.
