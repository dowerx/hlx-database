Overview

Tyler’s central item is a Valve audio-engineering talk at the Game Sound Convention in Burbank on October 21, 2026, where a panelist says the HLX codename out loud — which Tyler presents as the first direct public utterance of it. He pairs that with roughly 30 hours of datamining across three engine-level updates released two weeks apart (CS2’s Rush Hour, Deadlock’s City Never Sleeps, and Dota 2’s Inside the Trove of Terror), walking through Hammer helpers for traps, cover, projectile emitters, and Xen dressing, plus gunship bombing, cloth, held-object fire rules, and precipitation audio. A Source Filmmaker 2 bone-classification change for a “Gen Biped” rig, reported by dataminer Kayla, is read as further MetaHuman-adjacent work. The back half argues from Valve’s public commit-number graph that the studio is in a productivity surge, concedes AI use at Valve since 2024, and predicts HLX will not be announced in 2026. Tyler is explicit that datamined strings do not date the underlying code, and his HLX attributions are largely inference from strings that ship in other Valve games.
