Overview

Tyler frames this as the most active and unusual period he has observed at Valve, with Deadlock performing well in invite-only alpha while the company balances Counter-Strike 2, hardware work including the Deckard headset, SteamOS and Proton, residual Team Fortress 2 support, and a dedicated Half-Life 3 team of a few dozen people. The news hook is Valve’s hiring of most of Hopoo Games, whose unannounced project codenamed “Snail” was canceled; Tyler speculates that most of those hires will work on HLX rather than Deadlock. He also corrects the public impression that Half-Life 3 is open world, describing a wide-linear structure with procedural activities and voxel-based destruction. Most HLX specifics are presented without artifacts, so confidence is largely speculative, while the Hopoo hiring and cancellation are treated as reported fact.
