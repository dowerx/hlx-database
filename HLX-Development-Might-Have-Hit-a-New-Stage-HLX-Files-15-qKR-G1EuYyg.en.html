Overview

After roughly a two-month hiatus, Tyler returns with a mixed batch of datamine findings, hoax debunkings, and hardware context. He argues that recent Counter-Strike 2 update strings — destructible hit groups, destruction death behavior, liquid spills, and sound absorption — represent late-stage refactoring and optimization of systems whose functions have existed for about two years, while the new “science assets” icon is treated as either an older stray asset or unrelated to HLX. He dismisses the 4chan concept-art drop as AI-generated and the Mike Shapiro “Valve opening” story as invented, explains away a Half-Life 2 SteamDB changelist as a mod update, and relays a hearsay rumor that trailer work is underway. Overall he is confident about the datamine-derived facts but explicitly hedges the timeline and trailer claims, tentatively pointing to November for a possible Steam Frame announcement rather than a Game Awards reveal.

Sound absorption refactor in the surface property system

  • Description: Absorption coefficients in code refer to sound absorption, not liquid/material absorption — the dampening effect of materials on sound waves. Previously separate surface attributes were set per frequency band (high, mid, low); now sound absorption, scattering, and transmission based on material properties are refactored into one cleaner, cheaper, more optimized system. Tyler notes absorption and transmission no longer use separate frequency band ranges.
  • Confidence: Mixed — the refactor is evidenced by the datamined strings; the claim that it is an optimization step and how it behaves is Tyler’s interpretation.

Liquid spill strings and expiration-time optimization

  • Description: Strings include “C_OP_game_liquid_spill,” a C particle function renderer, “liquid contents” (negative for water, positive for oil, zero for none), “M_FL expiration time” for contribution amount, and a particle field scalar amount attribute. Tyler explains that expiration time controls how long spilled liquid is rendered/simulated before despawning, and that the amount field covers quantities from a gallon to a swimming pool to an eyedropper. He speculates the game will feature odd oil/water/wood/metal/fire physics puzzles that may grow tedious on replays, “like the seesaw puzzle, but for a new generation.”
  • Confidence: Mixed — the strings and their literal meanings are datamined evidence; the puzzle-design expectation is speculation.
