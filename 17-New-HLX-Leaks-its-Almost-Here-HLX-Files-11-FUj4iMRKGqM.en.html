Overview

Tyler skims what he considers the most important HLX-related findings from a Deadlock update and a Dota 2 update released over the previous month and a half. The findings center on a from-scratch Source 2 save/load rewrite, vehicle transport and meshlet attributes, electrical and elemental simulation, multithreaded physics, asynchronous AI, crouch/crawl states, expanded sight-and-sound AI, new entity flags, and a wind/fan system. He frames the work as late-cycle polish and says an announcement could come any day or a year from now, while explicitly discouraging expectations for Summer Game Fest. He also criticizes press outlets for attributing a Team Fortress 3 claim to him that he says he never made.
